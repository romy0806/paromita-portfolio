BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Why This Project?

The Problem

Marketing teams often review campaign results, customer journeys, attribution models, and experiments in separate tools. This makes it difficult to reconcile conflicting signals, determine what influenced performance, and communicate a clear recommendation to stakeholders.

The Goal

Build one practical measurement workspace that converts uploaded marketing data into transparent performance analysis, statistical evidence, budget scenarios, and client-ready reports without requiring users to understand the implementation details.

SOLUTION OVERVIEW

From Marketing Data to Decisions

Users can explore the synthetic demonstration data immediately or upload campaign performance, customer journey, and experiment CSV files. Date and channel filters update the KPIs, charts, models, decision support, and exported reports consistently.

The community edition uses a deterministic Python decision engine so the public demo remains useful without exposing or billing an API key. Recommendations are grounded in observed efficiency, model evidence, uncertainty, and practical measurement guardrails.

MEASUREMENT APPROACH

Triangulation Instead of a Single “Perfect” Answer

The application treats MMM, attribution, and experiments as complementary evidence. MMM estimates aggregate contribution, attribution describes measured journey credit, and experiments provide the strongest evidence of incrementality when properly designed.

Model disagreement is presented as a diagnostic signal rather than hidden. The decision engine recommends controlled changes and validation before scaling, helping users avoid treating correlation as causation.
