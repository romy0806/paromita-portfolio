SOLUTION OVERVIEW

How the Predictive Model Works

The solution transforms raw healthcare claims data into patient-level features that describe prescription history, medical events, provider interactions, and utilization patterns. These engineered variables are used to estimate the probability of a patient progressing to Long-Term Opioid Therapy (LTOT).

Multiple machine learning approaches were evaluated—including Logistic Regression, LASSO, Decision Trees, and Random Forest—to identify the model that best balanced predictive accuracy and clinical interpretability. Random Forest achieved the strongest overall performance and was selected as the final model for risk prediction.

MODELING APPROACH

Predictive Analytics Workflow

🧹 Data Preparation

Restructured claims data from multiple event-level records into one patient-level record and engineered ratio-based variables to normalize differences in member history.

⚙️ Feature Selection

Reduced the original 210 variables using stepwise regression, LASSO, and model-based importance to identify the strongest predictors of long-term opioid therapy.

🧠 Model Comparison

Evaluated Logistic Regression, LASSO, Elastic Net, Decision Trees, and Random Forest using ROC-AUC as the primary selection metric.

📊 Risk Scoring

Produced patient-level probabilities of progressing to long-term opioid therapy and ranked members for potential early intervention.

MODEL PERFORMANCE

Comparing Machine Learning Models

Multiple predictive models were evaluated to identify the best-performing approach for estimating Long-Term Opioid Therapy (LTOT) risk. Random Forest delivered the strongest overall predictive performance while maintaining high sensitivity and specificity.

BUSINESS IMPACT

Supporting Earlier Intervention and Better Clinical Decisions

The model was designed to help insurers and healthcare providers identify members at elevated risk of Long-Term Opioid Therapy before opioid use becomes chronic. Earlier risk identification can support more informed prescribing decisions, targeted intervention, and potentially lower downstream treatment costs.

Earlier Risk Identification

Patient-level risk probabilities can help clinicians and insurers prioritize members who may benefit from additional review or preventive intervention.

Clinical Decision Support

The model provides an additional data-driven signal to support opioid prescribing decisions alongside clinical judgment.

Potential Cost Savings

The case-study cost-benefit analysis estimated approximately $30,600 in savings across a hypothetical sample of 1,400 patients.
